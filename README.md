# 告白Confession

To the singles who come here for hopes, I will have to extinguish that hope right there.

It's not that you have no hope, but rather the opposite gender has too high expectations.

I myself have yet to find one despite creating this.

This is a V2 evolution of [Will You Be My Valentine?](https://github.com/StevenX-SG/Will-you-be-my-valentine), rebuilt from the ground up after Manus raised the bar on the confession game trend. It was inspired by all the viral couples' date night reels on Instagram/Manus Date ideas, built using [@hohjiada](https://www.instagram.com/hohjiada/)'s concept, and based on [will-you-be-my-valentine](https://github.com/saurabhnemade/will-you-be-my-valentine).

---

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Confession Game:** The evasive "No" button dodges clicks while leading to a cute "Yes" moment.
- **On-Page Link Generator:** Create custom proposal links directly in the browser with sender & recipient names.
- **Date & Venue Picker:** Allows the recipient to choose the date and venue once they accept.
- **Sharable Confirmation Pass:** Generates a ticket pass for the sender once the date is confirmed.
- **Interactive GIF Reactions:** High-energy reactions throughout the experience.
- **Fully Responsive:** Smooth UI optimized for mobile devices and desktops.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Create a Proposal:** Open the main generator view, type your name and your crush's name, and generate your custom link.
2. **Send the Link:** Send your generated link to your crush.
3. **Interactive Flow:** They dodge the "No" button, click "Yes", and select their preferred date and venue.
4. **Get the Confirmation:** They send you back the generated acceptance link to reveal your official "Date Pass" ticket!

## 🕵️ How to Hide the GitHub URL (Keep it a Surprise!)

If you send a direct `github.io` link, your recipient might guess what the app is before opening it. Here are two ways to mask your link for free:

### Option 1: Quick & Easy (Use a URL Shortener)

1. Generate your custom link using the on-page generator (e.g., `https://stevenx-sg.github.io/Confession/?from=Alex&to=Sarah`).
2. Paste that link into a free shortener like [TinyURL](https://tinyurl.com) or [Dub.co](https://dub.co).
3. Send the shortened link (`https://tinyurl.com/x92k-m821`) to your crush!

### Option 2: Advanced (Deploy Your Own Private Copy)

If you want your own custom domain (like `https://alex-and-sarah.vercel.app`) with zero mention of GitHub:

1. Click the **Deploy with Vercel** button below.
2. Vercel will clone this repository into your account and give you a free, private URL in under 60 seconds!
